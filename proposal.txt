InstaInventory – Complete Product Spec and Tech Guide
1. Problem and Opportunity
1.1 The Reality in Egypt Today
Egyptian micro-brands (fashion, cosmetics, accessories) run their entire business through Instagram DMs and WhatsApp chats, not websites.

Current workflow:

Post product photos/stories on Instagram

Get "Price?" / "Available in M?" DMs

Negotiate price/availability in chat

Take address/payment details via WhatsApp

Track everything in Notes app, Excel, or memory

Resulting pains:

Overselling popular sizes/colors

Forgotten orders buried in 100+ DMs

No stock visibility across conversations

Manual chaos as they scale from 5→50 orders/day

1.2 The Solution: InstaInventory
Simple inventory-first SaaS that turns Instagram/WhatsApp chats into structured orders with accurate stock tracking.

Positioning: "Never oversell on Instagram again. Track stock + orders for your IG/WhatsApp store in one Arabic dashboard."

Key differentiator: Not payments-first (like Zbooni) or automation-heavy (like Intenrix). Inventory accuracy first.

1.3 Why This Fits You Perfectly
✅ You already researched Egyptian chat-commerce behaviors

✅ Egyptian context (COD culture, WhatsApp ubiquity, IG dominance)

✅ Frontend skills perfect for dashboard + checkout pages

✅ Micro-SaaS scale: 50→500 paying merchants = real revenue

3. Product Workflows
3.1 Core Principles
text
1. DMs happen where they already happen (IG/WhatsApp apps)
2. Your app = BACK-OFFICE (products, stock, orders)
3. DM → copy customer → build cart → paste checkout link → done
4. Arabic-first, mobile-first, COD-first
3.2 Seller Onboarding (5 minutes)
text
1. Sign up (email/password)
2. Create shop:
   - Shop name, logo, WhatsApp number
   - Currency: EGP (fixed)
3. Add first products:
   - Name, price, variants (size/color), stock qty
   - 1-2 photos each
3.3 Daily Workflow (Manual v1)
text
DM conversation: "Want black dress size M, Nasr City"
    ↓
Seller opens InstaInventory (PWA on phone)
    ↓  
1. Find/create customer (by phone)
2. New Order → add "Dress / M-Black" (2 pcs)
3. Add EGP 50 shipping → Total EGP 550
4. "Generate checkout link" → https://instainventory.app/c/abc123
    ↓
Copy link → paste in DM
    ↓
Buyer clicks → fills address → order appears in dashboard
    ↓
Seller: Mark Confirmed → stock deducted automatically
3.4 Buyer Experience
text
Public page: instainventory.app/checkout/abc123
✅ Shows: shop logo, items, total, shipping breakdown
✅ Collects: name, WhatsApp, address (city/area/street)
✅ Payment: COD or "mark as paid" 
✅ Submit → "Order received! #123"
